# Playa continua · acuarelas v2

Se conserva la arena y el mar de `arena-continua-v1.webp` y se retiran de la copia la portada de cielo rosado, las toallas anteriores, el avión de línea y las copas de línea. La portada muestra exactamente cinco sillas vacías, una sombrilla y un velo solo en la silla central. Las nuevas ilustraciones se distribuyen en tamaños pequeños y alternan su posición entre secciones.

Se actualiza únicamente la copia `aab30e94-bd25-4fac-8982-36d513cbc31d`. Se conservan textos, fechas, enlaces, música, contador y publicación en borrador. La invitación original a rayas no cambia.

## Prompts

Cada uno de los prompts específicos se envió seguido literalmente por este estilo común:

```text
Style for the entire set: very loose real WATERCOLOR on cold-pressed paper, translucent watery pigment, wet-on-wet blooms, feathered imperfect soft edges, colors bleeding slightly into each other, sparse intentional brushstrokes, minimal definition. Soft denim/seafoam blue, pale aqua, warm sand ochre, muted coral and tiny sage accents. Airy small stationery illustration, playful and hand painted. Absolutely NO crisp pen or pencil outlines, NO opaque gouache poster blocks, NO realistic 3D rendering, NO photographic lighting, NO glossy reflections, NO intricate texture, NO tightly modeled details, NO dense invented paper texture. Objects are suggested with a few watery brushstrokes, not rendered meticulously. Genuinely TRANSPARENT outside the watercolor shapes; no rectangular paper background, no sea or sky scene, no grey background, no drop-shadow halo, no lettering, no brands, no people. Soft residual peach strokes beneath an object may be extremely faint but must dissolve to transparent.
```

### sillas-velo-acuarela-v2.webp

```text
Edit target: the supplied earlier watercolor of five empty beach chairs and one blue/cream umbrella. Retain EXACTLY FIVE chairs in a naturally spaced row viewed from behind, facing the implied sea. Keep ONE single umbrella on the left. Repaint more simply and loosely, less dense surface texture. The five striped chair fabrics can be muted yellow, sage, coral, pale blue, dusty rose; simple small warm wood frames. Add a sheer WHITE BRIDAL VEIL hanging down the back of ONLY THE CENTRAL (third) chair, trailing just a little to the sand, visibly airy and translucent; all other four chairs have NO veil. No bride or people, no hats, no additional chairs or parasols. Remove ALL background paper and sea and horizon so the row of five chairs plus one umbrella plus the one bridal veil is a transparent cutout that can be placed on an existing watercolor beach background. All five chairs fully visible and countable, not cropped. Wide horizontal composition, no large empty space above.
```
